# USER

Semantic model of the Owner — preferences, patterns, communication
tells. Hard cap is **tier-dependent** (lean 60 / standard 100 /
extended 200; consolidate at 80% of active cap) — see
`3-control/runtime/profile.md`.

Loaded at session-start (item 5). Frozen-at-session-start: entries
written here appear in the *next* session, not the current.

## Preferences

Decision / style / format preferences the Owner consistently shows.
Example: prefers menu-format options; picks fix-all over per-item.

## Patterns

Recurring workflows or framings. Example: research → narrow options
→ pick → ratify plan → execute.

## Tells

Communication signals — what specific phrasings mean. Example: "fix
all" means exact list, no scope creep; "do (a)" means option a
verbatim; silence on options means "you decide".
